CROATIA ITINERARY
Split, Hvar & Dubrovnik — 9 days

DAY 1 — Fly to Croatia
  • Overnight flight to Dubrovnik (DBV)

DAY 2 — Split
  • Drive from Dubrovnik to Split (about 3.5 hours), stopping for lunch on the way
  • Check in and freshen up
  • Walk the Riva Promenade
  • Diocletian's Palace and the Peristyle
  • Gregory of Nin statue
  • Dinner and drinks in Old Town

DAY 3 — Krka National Park
  • Full-day tour of Krka National Park (Skradinski Buk falls, swim stop)
  • Downtime: nap, explore town, rooftop drinks or Bačvice Beach
  • Quick dinner at Davinci Focaccia
  • Night out in Split

DAY 4 — Ferry to Hvar
  • Optional morning: Bačvice Beach or climb the Cathedral of Saint Domnius bell tower
  • Midday ferry from Split to Hvar
  • Lunch near St. Stephen's Square and check in
  • Walk up to Fortica Španjola
  • Drinks along the harbor
  • Dinner at Don Quijote
  • Drinks at Kiva Bar

DAY 5 — Hvar Boat Day
  • Full-day private boat around the Pakleni Islands (cliff jumping, swim stops)
  • Dinner and a night at Carpe Diem Beach Club

DAY 6 — Hvar
  • Explore Old Town: St. Stephen's Square and the Franciscan Monastery
  • Lunch, swim and lounge at Beach Club Hvar
  • Sunset dinner at Hula Hula Beach Club

DAY 7 — Ferry to Dubrovnik
  • Morning ferry from Hvar to Dubrovnik
  • Drop bags and lunch at Franco Romano
  • Wander Old Town: the Stradun, Jesuit Staircase, Rector's Palace, the Cathedral and the shops on Ulica od Puča
  • Optional drinks in Old Town
  • Dinner at Rudjer

DAY 8 — Dubrovnik
  • City Walls walk (includes Fort Lovrijenac)
  • Lunch in Old Town; optional Game of Thrones tour
  • Buža Bar: cliff jumping, swimming and drinks
  • Optional cable car up Mount Srđ
  • Dinner at Konoba

DAY 9 — Fly home
  • Breakfast nearby and check out
  • Flight home from Dubrovnik (DBV)
